ABOUT ME

Bridging healthcare needs and technology.

My professional focus is at the intersection of healthcare operations, digital product development, and project execution. I am interested in how technology can support better workflows, more reliable information, and more effective decision-making across healthcare organizations.

My areas of interest include hospital information systems, electronic medical records, healthcare data integration, and operational systems such as inventory and logistics.

I value clear requirements, collaboration between business and technical teams, and implementation plans that reflect the realities of healthcare environments.
